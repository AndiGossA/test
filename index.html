What's covered

Four pillars

Everything in the plan rolls up into one of these areas, so nothing is tracked twice and nothing falls through.

01

Budgeting

A monthly budget framework, emergency fund and savings milestones, and a tracking routine that runs from weekly to quarterly.

02

Personal goals

Health and wellbeing, relationships, and personal enrichment — with a structured check-in every quarter.

03

Career development

Current versus target state, promotion and networking milestones, and a quarter-by-quarter action plan.

04

Skill development

A skills audit, a learning plan costed against the budget, and concrete milestones through to a final review.

Keeping it honest

Review cadence

The plan only works if it stays current. Four loops, each with a clear job.

Weekly
Budget versus actuals, and the immediate to-dos for the week ahead.
Monthly
Progress against goals, accounts reconciled, next month's budget adjusted.
Quarterly
A full review across all four pillars, with goals re-prioritised where needed.
Annually
A complete plan reset — December 2026, then December 2027.
