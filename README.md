# Lifestyle App

A personal lifestyle planning project.

## Contents

- [`lifestyle-plan.md`](./lifestyle-plan.md) — Personal roadmap from August 2026
  through the end of 2027, covering budgeting, personal goals, career
  development, and skill development. Reviewed monthly and updated each
  quarter.

## Usage

Open `lifestyle-plan.md` and fill in the placeholder fields (marked `___`)
with your own figures and goals. Follow the review cadence in Section 7 of
that document to keep it up to date.
